Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Jay B Tattoos ("we," "us," or "our") operates the JB Tattoos website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a booking request (name, phone number, email, tattoo details)
  • Send a message through our contact form (name, phone, email, message)
  • Communicate with us via phone, email, or Instagram

How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely to:

  • Respond to booking requests and schedule appointments
  • Communicate about your tattoo consultation, design, and session
  • Process deposit payments and manage appointment logistics
  • Reply to inquiries submitted through the contact form

Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Web3Forms — processes form submissions. Your form data is transmitted securely to Web3Forms for email delivery. See their privacy policy at web3forms.com.
  • Google Fonts — serves typefaces used on this site. Google may collect anonymized usage data. See Google's privacy policy for details.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your booking and contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill your appointment and provide follow-up care instructions. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Cookies & Tracking

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools. We do not track your browsing behavior.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time

Portfolio Images

Photos of completed tattoo work displayed on this site are used with client consent. If you are a client and would like your tattoo photo removed from the portfolio, please contact us.
